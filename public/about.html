Our Culinary Heritage

The Art of Wood-Smoke & Spice

Founded on an obsession with authentic charcoal heat and slow wood-smoked meats, Cravers Smokehouse was built to bring world-class pitmaster standards to Nigerian street food and executive catering.

🪵

Slow Hardwood Smoke

We cure and smoke our flank steaks, chicken quarters, and brisket over natural hickory and oak logs for up to 14 hours for deeply infused aroma and melt-in-the-mouth tenderness.

🥩

100% Halal Prime Cuts

No fillers, zero artificial tenderizers, and no imported scrap meats. We source certified fresh poultry and prime beef cuts daily from local ethical farms.

🛵

Thermal Heat Dispatch

Our specialized delivery fleet operates express corridors with thermal insulated bags, ensuring every shawarma arrives steaming hot in under 40 minutes.
